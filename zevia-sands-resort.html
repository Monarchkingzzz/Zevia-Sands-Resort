The Setting

Sun, Palms & Shimmering Waters

Spend sun-filled afternoons between our freshwater infinity lagoon and a secluded two-kilometer private white sand beach.

The 40m Lagoon Infinity Pool

Submerged stone sun loungers, heated crystal-clear water, and shaded palm cabanas with all-day fruit and chilled cocktail service.

Powdery soft sand protected by an outer coral reef barrier, ensuring calm, warm, turquoise waters ideal for all-day swimming.
