Who We Are

A Secluded Sanctuary on Kalmara Bay

Zevia Sands Resort is an eco-conscious luxury retreat on the sun-kissed coast of Diani, Kenya. Shielded by an ancient natural barrier reef, our bay is blessed with calm, warm turquoise water 365 days a year.

Unlike massive commercial hotel complexes, every structure here was designed to sit lightly on the earth. Thatch-roofed overwater pavilions, native coral stone walls, and reclaimed coastal timber honor Swahili coastal heritage while delivering world-class modern comfort.

The Genesis

Born from a Passion for Unhurried Living

In 2018, a team of coastal architects and marine conservationists stood on this quiet cove and envisioned a place where travelers could disconnect from the noise of the modern world without sacrificing luxury.

Our philosophy is rooted in "Barefoot Elegance, Sustainable Soul". 100% of our energy is solar-supplemented, single-use plastics are completely eliminated, and our in-house marine biologist leads daily turtle conservation initiatives on the reef.

The Difference

What Sets Zevia Sands Apart
