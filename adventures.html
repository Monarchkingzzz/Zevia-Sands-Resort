Expeditions & Restoration

Ocean Adventures & Wellness

Explore vibrant marine sanctuaries, glide across mirror-calm turquoise shallows, and rejuvenate with beachfront botanical wellness.

Lagoon Discovery

100% Transparent Glass Kayaking

Glide effortlessly across the pristine turquoise waters of Kalmara Bay in tandem kayaks made entirely from crystal-clear marine polycarbonate. Observe live coral gardens, starfish, and tropical schools beneath your feet.

⏱️ All-Day Access 🛶 Tandem & Solo ✨ Included with Stay
Marine Safari

Sea Turtle Reef Snorkel Safari

Take a 10-minute guided dhoni boat ride to the outer marine sanctuary barrier reef. Snorkel alongside gentle green sea turtles, rays, and kaleidoscopic marine life under the supervision of our resident marine biologist.

⏱️ Daily at 09:00 & 14:00 🤿 Gear & Guide Provided 🐢 Sanctuary Protected
Evening Sail

Sunset Champagne Catamaran Cruise

Board our 50ft luxury sailing catamaran as the sun dips below the horizon in dazzling amber and rose. Enjoy vintage champagne, fresh oyster platters, canapés, and gentle acoustic island melodies.

⏱️ 17:00 – 19:30 Daily 🍾 Champagne & Tapas ⛵ 50ft Luxury Vessel
Holistic Spa

Beachside Palm Coconut Oil Massage

Surrender to rhythmic ocean waves under the shade of rustling coconut fronds. Full-body massage utilizing cold-pressed island coconut oil, fragrant frangipani, and warm soothing herbal compresses.

⏱️ 60 or 90 Minutes 🥥 Organic Coconut Elixirs 🏖️ Private Beach Pavilion
