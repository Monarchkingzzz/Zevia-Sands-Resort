Frequently Asked Questions

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 onwards, and check-out is at 11:00. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to villa availability.

How do we arrange airport transfers?

Complimentary private boat transfer is included for stays of 3 nights or more. Our airport concierge welcomes you directly at arrival.

Are dietary requirements catered for?

Yes. Our executive chefs curate bespoke plant-based, gluten-free, halal, and custom tasting menus tailored to your preferences.

What is the resort cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 14 days prior to arrival. Flexible re-booking options are provided for all direct reservations.
